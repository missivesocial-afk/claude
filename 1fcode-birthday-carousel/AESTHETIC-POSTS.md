# 1FCode: Rishabh M Shah birthday, editorial set (less AI, more designer)

5 single posts, 1080 x 1350 (4:5). No cut-out people this time: every post uses the real photographs with their real backgrounds, which is what makes them read as designed rather than generated. The photos are lightly colour-graded toward the 1FC palette (Legacy Green in the shadows, a little warmth in the highlights) and stay in full colour. Film grain and paper texture finish each one.

Files are in `aesthetic-post/`. To edit, change `aesthetic-post.html`, then run `python3 render.py aesthetic-post.html aesthetic-post`.

| File | Format | Photo | What makes it feel crafted |
|---|---|---|---|
| `a1-film-still.png` | Film still | Beige blazer | Letterbox bars in near-black green, lime subtitles in the bar ("Happy birthday, Rishabh. / Here's to the next chapter."), and "SCENE 16 · TAKE 01" for his 16 years. |
| `a2-journal-cover.png` | Magazine cover | Chair | A full-bleed portrait against the warm tiled wall. Masthead: "The 1FC Journal · Birthday Issue". Serif headline: "Happy birthday, Rishabh." A lime "16 years in banking & investment" tag. |
| `a3-contact-sheet.png` | Photographer's contact sheet | All three | A real film strip with sprocket holes and frame numbers. Frame 16 is circled in lime marker with "frame 16. this one ✓", and the headline is handwritten. |
| `a4-gallery-wall.png` | Gallery wall | Leaning | The photo hung in a Legacy Green frame with a white mat. Exhibition title: "Happy birthday, Rishabh." Museum label: "The Founder, 2026 · Collection of 1FC Group of Companies · Not for sale. Priceless." |
| `a5-book-cover.png` | Classic three-band book cover | Beige blazer | "1FC Classics: Rishabh, or, How to Stay Bullish for 16 Years · A Birthday Edition, with love from Team 1FC". |

Our picks: **A3 contact sheet** (most original, uses all three photos) and **A1 film still** (most cinematic).
