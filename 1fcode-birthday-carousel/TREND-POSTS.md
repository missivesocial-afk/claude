# 1FCode: Rishabh M Shah birthday, trend-led single posts

8 single posts, 1080 x 1350 (4:5). Each one takes a style trending in 2026 and keeps only the 1FC palette: Legacy Green `#043335`, Light Lime `#EAE26A`, Bond White `#F4F4ED`, and the gradient between them.

Files are in `trend-post/`. To edit, change `trend-post.html`, then run `python3 render.py trend-post.html trend-post`.

| # | File | Trend it rides | The idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | `t1-scrapbook.png` | Canva 2026 "Notes App Chic", scrapbook collage, 2000s flash photo | A torn note, a flash Polaroid with an "HBD" date stamp, washi tape, a Notes-app to-do list ("16 years in banking ✓, magazine cover ✓, cut the cake ☐"), his magazine cover clipped in, and doodles. |
| T2 | `t2-neo-brutal.png` | Neo-brutalism: hard shadows, stickers, ticker tape | "NEW LEVEL UNLOCKED" (a play on the keyhole), his photo in an app window, a "16 YRS" starburst, a spinning FOUNDER ✦ CEO badge and crossed ticker tapes. |
| T3 | `t3-riso-zine.png` | Riso and zine print (Canva: zine layouts +85%, lo-fi +527%) | A halftone portrait with a lime offset print, overprinted "HAPPY BIRTH DAY", registration marks, "The Birthday Zine Nº 01". |
| T4 | `t4-pixel-stretch.png` | Instagram's pixel-stretch portrait edit, in a duotone | His portrait mapped onto the brand gradient, smeared into vertical streaks. The giant "RISHABH" sits behind his head, with a news-style name bar. |
| T5 | `t5-lime-chrome.png` | Restrained Y2K chrome, Pinterest's loud "Glamoratti" maximalism | Lime-chrome bubble lettering, a glowing rim light, sparkles and a chrome name pill. |
| T6 | `t6-bento.png` | Bento grid | Tiles: "Today's main character" photo, vertical HAPPY BIRTHDAY, a 16-years stat, the magazine cover, the keyhole candle, his name and title, "love, Team 1FC". |
| T7 | `t7-aura-glass.png` | Aura / mesh gradient + frosted glass | A soft, grainy brand-gradient aura with a glass card, a glowing portrait ring and glass pills. |
| T8 | `t8-lock-screen.png` | Phone-UI posts + the lock-screen depth effect | A lock screen where the huge "HBD" clock sits behind his head, plus "Team 1FC" and "Calendar" birthday notifications. |

**Our pick for maximum stop-power:** T4 Pixel Stretch or T1 Scrapbook. For the most on-brand while still trendy: T3 Riso Zine.
