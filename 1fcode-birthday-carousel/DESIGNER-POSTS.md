## What we took from the best founder birthday posts

- **One idea, done well.** Tata Group's tribute to Ratan Tata was a smiling photo and one line. N5 and N6 follow that restraint, kept in full colour and celebratory because Rishabh is very much with us.
- **Play on the name.** Amul's "Poore vatan ka Ratan" played on Ratan Tata's name. "Rishabh" literally means bull in Sanskrit, so N1, R1 and R2 make him "born bullish".
- **The business becomes the celebration.** The best brand creatives turn their own product into the occasion. Here a banknote, a metal card, a portfolio chart and a compounding formula all become birthday cards.
- **Keep it human.** Nithin Kamath's note for Nikhil's birthday was personal and funny. The scrapbook's to-do list ("Cut the cake ☐") carries that tone.
