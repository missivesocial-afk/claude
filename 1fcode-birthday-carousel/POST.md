## What the best founder birthday posts do (and how this one applies it)

1. **The founder's face on the cover, no clip art.** Balloons and confetti read as a template. A real portrait in brand colours reads as respect.
2. **A story, not a CV.** The posts that get the most comments tell why the founder started. Here that's slide 2.
3. **Gratitude, not bragging.** Achievements are framed as proof of the mission (slide 5), not as a trophy shelf.
4. **Brand-native design.** The founder sits inside the logo's brackets, so the cover is recognisably 1FC even without the logo.
5. **One genuine ask.** "Leave your wishes" is a natural prompt on a birthday post. It isn't engagement bait.
6. **Something worth saving.** Slide 7 gives viewers a reason to keep the post after the day has passed.
