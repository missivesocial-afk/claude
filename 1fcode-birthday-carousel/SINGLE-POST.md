# 1FCode: Rishabh M Shah birthday, single-post concepts

7 single-post options, each 1080 x 1350 (4:5), all built from the 1FC brand guide: Legacy Green `#043335`, Light Lime `#EAE26A`, Bond White `#F4F4ED`, the brand gradient, and the bracket-and-keyhole mark.

Files are in `single-post/`. To edit, change `single-post.html`, then run `python3 render.py single-post.html single-post`.

## Posting notes

- **Post it as an Instagram Collab with Rishabh's personal account.** It then appears on both profiles and shares one set of likes and comments. For a founder post, this is the biggest reach lever you have.
- Post on the morning of his birthday, IST (9 to 11 am), and reply to every wish in the first hour.
- Share it to Stories with a "Send Rishabh a wish" question sticker that links to the post.
- Concept 04 uses "RMS" as a playful ticker made from his initials. Because 1FC offers stock broking, drop the chip if your compliance team would rather avoid anything that looks like a stock symbol.
