## What changed from the last round

1. **Contrast:** the dark suit always sits on lime or white, the light blazer on dark green. Never dark on dark.
2. **One colour drives each post** (60 to 80% of the area), with one high-contrast accent, as in all four references.
3. **Real, sharp photos** cropped at the frame edge or standing on a ground line. No faded-out bodies.
4. **The brand does a job:** he leans on the 1FC bracket, rests his hands on a 1FC ledge, or leans on a 1FC chart.
